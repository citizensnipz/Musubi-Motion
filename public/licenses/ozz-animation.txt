Musubi Motion adapts the two-bone quaternion correction algorithm to scalar
Three.js operations in src/two-bone-ik.ts. It uses full reach without softening,
no twist offset, external IK/FK blending, and explicit deterministic fallbacks
for coincident targets and collinear poles.
